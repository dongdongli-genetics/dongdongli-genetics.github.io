Research Interests

Plant breeding, molecular genetics, quantitative genetics, and statistical genomics.

Current Research Projects

  • Flowering-time plasticity: studying how cis-regulatory variation contributes to environmental response.
  • Environmental adaptation: using CERIS-based models to identify environmental indices for yield plasticity.
  • Maize heterosis: dissecting hybrid performance and yield-related heterosis using diallel populations.

Skills

  • Field phenotyping and crop trial management.
  • R and Linux-based data analysis workflows.
  • QTL mapping, genomic selection, GWAS, and RNA-seq analysis.
  • NGS tools including Vcftools, PLINK, Beagle, ADMIXTURE, and TASSEL.
  • Selection-scan methods including pi, Fst, XP-CLR, and XP-EHH.
  • Primer design and polymerase chain reaction (PCR).
