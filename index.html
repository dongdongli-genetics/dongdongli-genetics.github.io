About

Plant breeding | quantitative genetics | statistical genomics

I am a postdoctoral research associate in Dr. Jianming Yu's group in the Department of Agronomy at Iowa State University. My research integrates plant breeding, quantitative genetics, statistical genomics, and high-throughput phenotyping to study complex traits in maize.

My current work focuses on genotype-by-environment interaction, phenotypic plasticity, environmental adaptation, and the genetic architecture of heterosis. I am especially interested in connecting breeding questions with genomic data, environmental information, and quantitative models.

  • Genomic prediction and selection in structured breeding populations.
  • Genotype-by-environment interaction and maize adaptation across environments.
  • Heterosis, reciprocal crosses, pan-genome resources, and hybrid performance.
