About

Plant breeding | quantitative genetics | statistical genomics

Li's research addresses fundamental questions in plant breeding by integrating quantitative genetics, statistical genomics, and emerging technologies to investigate complex quantitative traits. His work focuses on nutrient- and water-use efficiency, phenotypic plasticity, crop heterosis, and strategies for harnessing genetic diversity preserved in genebanks.

Collectively, this research elucidates the genetic and environmental determinants of phenotypic plasticity and their interplay, while informing parental-line selection, hybrid development, and the use of global germplasm in crop improvement.

Molecular breeding for nutrient- and water-use efficiency in maize

Proposed breeding strategies center on genomic selection, GWAS-identified loci, existing genetic information, and molecular breeding tools to develop maize inbred lines and hybrids with improved nutrient- and water-use efficiency (Front. Agric. Sci. Eng., 2019; Euphytica, 2021; PLOS ONE, 2021; JXB, 2021, 2022; IJMS, 2021, 2022, 2024, 2025; Frontiers in Plant Science, 2022; The Crop Journal, 2023; Plant Biotechnology Journal, 2025).

Genomic selection contributing to breeding strategies

Designed genomic selection frameworks to improve prediction accuracy and enable efficient implementation in plant breeding programs, thereby accelerating genetic gain (The Plant Genome, 2019, 2025; PLOS ONE, 2019; Plant Breeding, 2019; Plants, 2021).

Hybrid performance and heterosis in plant breeding

Developed statistical genomics frameworks and inbred-line development strategies to advance understanding of hybrid performance and heterosis and to inform hybrid breeding (Frontiers in Plant Science, 2021; Crop Science, 2024; Journal of Advanced Research, 2025).

Phenotypic plasticity and genotype-by-environment interactions

Phenotypic plasticity is the ability of a single genotype to produce different phenotypes in response to environmental conditions. Genetic variation in plasticity gives rise to genotype-by-environment interactions. This work reveals the genetic basis of genotype-by-environment-by-management interactions and advances our understanding of how environmental response operates under field conditions (IJMS, 2022).
