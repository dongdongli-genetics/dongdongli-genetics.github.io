Dec 18, 2024

Career in Academia Workshop

I joined the Career in Academia Workshop hosted by the Raymond F. Baker Center for Plant Breeding. Dr. Patrick Schnable, Dr. Jianming Yu, and Dr. Kendall Lamkey shared career experiences and discussed academic paths with graduate students and postdocs.

Nov 15, 2024

Congratulations to Yanting

Congratulations to Yanting on the publication "ZmCCD8 regulates sugar and amino acid accumulation in maize kernels via strigolactone signalling." This study provides new insight into carbon and nitrogen accumulation in maize kernels.

Jan 12-17, 2024

PAG 31 in San Diego: QTL mapping

At PAG 31, I had the opportunity to meet Dr. Zhaobang Zeng from NC State University, whose work on composite interval mapping has strongly influenced QTL analysis in biparental populations.

Jan 12-17, 2024

PAG 31 in San Diego: GWAS and GAPIT

I also met Dr. Zhiwu Zhang from Washington State University, whose contributions include CMLM, FarmCPU, BLINK, and the GAPIT software platform for genome-wide association studies.
