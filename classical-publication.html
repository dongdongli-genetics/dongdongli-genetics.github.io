Classical Publications

Influential papers and books that shape my reading in quantitative genetics, plant breeding, heterosis, phenotypic plasticity, and genotype-by-environment interaction.

Genetic Effect

  • Fisher, R. A. Average excess and average effect of a gene substitution.
  • Falconer, D. S. A note on Fisher's average effect and average excess.
  • Bernardo, R. Breeding for Quantitative Traits in Plants, 3rd edition.
  • Xu, S. Quantitative Genetics.

Joint Regression Analysis

  • Yates, F., & Cochran, W. G. (1938). The analysis of groups of experiments.
  • Finlay, K. W., & Wilkinson, G. N. (1963). The analysis of adaptation in a plant-breeding programme.
  • Eberhart, S. A., & Russell, W. A. (1966). Stability parameters for comparing varieties.
  • Perkins, J. M., & Jinks, J. L. (1968). Environmental and genotype-environmental components of variability.
  • Miezan, K., Milliken, G. A., & Liang, G. H. (1979). Using regression coefficient as a stability parameter in plant breeding programs.

Heterosis

  • Cockerham, C. C. (1954). An extension of the concept of partitioning hereditary variance.
  • Kao, C. H., & Zeng, Z. B. (2002). Modeling epistasis of quantitative trait loci using Cockerham's model.
  • Melchinger, A. E., Utz, H. F., Piepho, H. P., Zeng, Z. B., & Schon, C. C. (2007). The role of epistasis in the manifestation of heterosis.
  • Wang, T., & Zeng, Z. B. (2006). Models and partition of variance for quantitative trait loci with epistasis and linkage disequilibrium.
  • Xu, S. (2013). Mapping quantitative trait loci by controlling polygenic background effects.
  • Jiang, Y., Schmidt, R. H., Zhao, Y., & Reif, J. C. (2017). A quantitative genetic framework highlights the role of epistatic effects for grain-yield heterosis in bread wheat.
  • Zeng, Z. B., De Siqueira Gesteira, G., Mo, L., Xiao, Y., & Yan, J. (2025). A theory of heterosis.

Phenotypic Plasticity and G-by-E Interaction

  • Via, S., Gomulkiewicz, R., De Jong, G., Scheiner, S. M., Schlichting, C. D., & Van Tienderen, P. H. (1995). Adaptive phenotypic plasticity.
  • Schlichting, C. D., & Pigliucci, M. (1993). Control of phenotypic plasticity via regulatory genes.
  • Nicotra, A. B., Atkin, O. K., Bonser, S. P., Davidson, A. M., Finnegan, E. J., Mathesius, U., et al. (2010). Plant phenotypic plasticity in a changing climate.
  • Lacaze, X., Hayes, P. M., & Korol, A. (2009). Genetics of phenotypic plasticity: QTL analysis in barley, Hordeum vulgare.
  • Des Marais, D. L., Hernandez, K. M., & Juenger, T. E. (2013). Genotype-by-environment interaction and plasticity.
  • Kraakman, A. T., Niks, R. E., Van den Berg, P. M., Stam, P., & Van Eeuwijk, F. A. (2004). Linkage disequilibrium mapping of yield and yield stability.
