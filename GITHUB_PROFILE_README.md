# Dongdong Li

Postdoc Research Associate in the Department of Agronomy at Iowa State University.

I work at the intersection of plant breeding, quantitative genetics, and statistical genomics. My
research focuses on maize complex traits, with current interests in genotype-by-environment
interaction, phenotypic plasticity, genomic prediction, heterosis, and hybrid performance.

## Current Focus

- Identifying genomic loci, environmental determinants, and their interplay in maize adaptation.
- Improving prediction and selection across breeding stages and multi-environment trials.
- Studying heterosis and reciprocal crosses using pan-genome resources and high-throughput phenotyping.
- Sharing useful resources for quantitative genetics, R, maize data, and plant breeding.

## Methods and Tools

GWAS | Genomic selection | Mixed models | Reaction norms | Multi-environment trials | R | BGLR | rrBLUP
